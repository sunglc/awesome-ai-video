## Image-to-Video Tools

- [Popcorn AI](https://popcornai.art) - Turn images into short-form videos for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok. Designed for content creators.
- [Runway Gen-3](https://runwayml.com) - Professional-grade image and text to video generation.
- [Kling AI](https://klingai.com) - High-fidelity image-to-video with strong motion consistency.
- [Stable Video Diffusion](https://stability.ai) - Open-source image-to-video model from Stability AI.
- [Pika](https://pika.art) - Creative video generation with text and image inputs.
- [KDA Firestarter AI Dance](https://kdafirestarterai.dev) - Independent unofficial AI: one photo → 13s 480p 21:9 solo dance (Paid from $7.99). Not affiliated with Riot Games or K/DA
- [A2O LITE NEON AI Dance](https://a2oliteai.org) - Independent AI: two photos → 15s 480p 16:9 duo neon dance with reference soundtrack (Paid from $7.99)
- [dime dime baby ai](https://dimedimebabyai.dev) - Independent AI: one photo → ~5s 480p 9:16 AI dance (Quince Classic or Party Groove) (Paid from $7.99)
- [PromptImage](https://promptimage.cloud) - Free AI image & video studio with a prompt library (signup credits, multi-model). Freemium
- [GrokVideo Studio](https://grokvideogenerator.dev) - Independent web app for text/image-to-video via Grok Imagine API (Free Trial, 20 credits). Not an official xAI or X product
- [Alors On Danse Video Maker](https://alorsondansevideo.com) - Independent AI: two photos → 13s studio dance video (Paid; not affiliated with Stromae/TikTok)
- [Bad Times AI](https://badtimesai.dev) - Independent AI dance effect: one photo → 14–15s portrait dance video (Paid from $7.99)
- [I Found You in the Crowd AI](https://ifoundyouinthecrowdai.com) - Independent AI scene effect: two photos → balcony/spotlight meeting video (Paid from $7.99)

## Text-to-Video Tools

- [Sora](https://openai.com/sora) - OpenAI's text-to-video model (limited access).
- [Veo](https://deepmind.google/technologies/veo/) - Google DeepMind's video generation model.
- [Runway Gen-3 Alpha](https://runwayml.com) - Text-to-video with cinematic quality.

## Video Editing AI

- [Descript](https://descript.com) - AI-powered video editing with text-based editing.
- [CapCut](https://capcut.com) - AI features for quick video editing and captions.
- [Opus Clip](https://opus.pro) - AI-powered clip extraction from long-form video.

## Short-Form Video Creation

Tools specifically designed for creating YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok content:

- [Popcorn AI](https://popcornai.art) - Image-to-video optimized for short-form social content.
- [InVideo AI](https://invideo.io) - AI video creation with templates.
- [Pictory](https://pictory.ai) - Turn articles into short videos.
